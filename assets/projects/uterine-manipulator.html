Overview

Laparoscopic hysterectomy requires continuous uterine repositioning throughout a multi-hour procedure. At CUHK T-Stone Robotics Institute, I led development of robotic uterine manipulation systems that automate tissue positioning with mechanical and soft RCM constraints.

Key features:

  • Motorized 3-DoF manipulation rod integrated with dual-linear RCM platform
  • Multi-DoF instruments supporting pitch, roll, and grasping motions
  • Soft RCM constraint design for safe interaction with deformable tissue
  • TwinCAT-based control and validation through laboratory and clinical experiments

System Integration

The project spans mechanical design, control implementation, and clinical workflow integration. Configurability analysis was performed to select optimal robot arm configurations for constrained laparoscopic hysterectomy tasks.
