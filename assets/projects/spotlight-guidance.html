Overview

Retinal microsurgery demands sub-millimeter accuracy, yet surgeons must simultaneously interpret depth from a single microscope view. This project introduces spotlight-based 3D instrument guidance: a micro-spotlight projected from the tool tip encodes tip-to-retina distance for autonomous navigation.

Results on the Steady-Hand Eye Robot (SHER):

  • Average vessel tracking error of 0.013 mm on retinal phantoms
  • Distance maintenance within 0.1 mm of desired range during automatic tracking
  • Significant improvement over manual and cooperative control alone
  • Published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (co-first author, 2021)

Experimental Setup

A light fiber integrated into the surgical instrument is driven by SHER under RCM constraints. The spotlight pattern is segmented and tracked in real time to guide instrument motion during vessel following tasks on plane and spherical phantoms.
