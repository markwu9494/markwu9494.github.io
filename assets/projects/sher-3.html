Overview

Steady-Hand Eye Robot 3.0 (SHER) — Johns Hopkins University. Developed a collaborative-control microsurgical robot for retinal surgery, responsible for mechatronics design, prototyping, and kinematics optimization.

Results:

  • 1 paper nominated for IEEE IROS Best Paper
  • 2 papers accepted by IEEE journals
  • IROS 2020 paper selected as Best Paper Finalist
