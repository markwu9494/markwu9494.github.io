Overview

Flexible RCM Serial Uterus Manipulation Arm — CUHK T-Stone Robotics Institute. Developed a compact 7-DoF arm for laparoscopic hysterectomy with high power-density modular actuators.

  • Actuators built with TQ motors, HD harmonic reducers, magnetic sensors, and brakes
  • Power density superior to comparable commercial products
  • Led full-cycle mechatronics design from concept to prototype
  • Technology transferred to HK medical robotics industry
