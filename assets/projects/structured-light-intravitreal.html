Overview

Intravitreal injection is one of the most common procedures in ophthalmology, yet manual execution remains sensitive to operator variability. This project develops a structured-light-based robotic system with pre-fixation to automate injection point localization and improve procedural consistency.

Key contributions include:

  • Structured-light sensing for precise ocular surface and injection site localization
  • Robotic pre-fixation workflow to stabilize the eye before needle insertion
  • Full pipeline from early robot design (ROBIO 2022) to journal publication (IEEE TBME, 2026)
