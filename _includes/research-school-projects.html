Research & Explorations

  • Steady-Hand Eye Robot 3.0 (SHER) — Johns Hopkins University

    Developed a collaborative-control microsurgical robot for retinal surgery. Responsible for mechatronics design, prototyping, and kinematics optimization. Proposed force-feedback control and visual servo for vessel tracking.

    Ophthalmic Robotics Cooperative Control RCM Mechanism IEEE IROS Best Paper Finalist

  • Uterus Manipulation Robot — CUHK T-Stone Robotics Institute

    Developed a novel uterus manipulation robot with a coupled dual-linear platform for mechanical RCM and multi-DoF instruments with pitch, roll, and grasping. Led team management, mechatronics, and TwinCAT-based control.

    Gynecologic Surgery Remote Center of Motion TwinCAT Control Clinical Validation

  • Flexible RCM Serial Uterus Manipulation Arm — CUHK

    Developed a compact 7-DoF arm for laparoscopic hysterectomy with high power-density modular actuators using TQ motors, HD harmonic reducers, magnetic sensors, and brakes. Technology transferred to HK medical robotics industry.

    7-DoF Manipulation Modular Actuators Harmonic Reducers Technology Transfer
