Overview

Uterus Manipulation Robot — CUHK T-Stone Robotics Institute. Developed a novel uterus manipulation robot with a coupled dual-linear platform for mechanical RCM and multi-DoF instruments with pitch, roll, and grasping capabilities.

Served as project leader for team management, mechatronics design, and TwinCAT-based control implementation. Achieved 2 clinical validations.
